Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 16, 2026

At WheelPicker, we believe that simple utility tools shouldn't cost you your digital privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent, client-side approach to running our free online random wheel spinner application.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

WheelPicker is built to be an entirely anonymous utility. There is no account creation, sign-up flow, or membership registration. Consequently, we do not collect or store personal parameters such as your name, email address, IP address, or geolocation parameters.

2. Client-Side Mechanics & Entries

Any strings, text lists, labels, or configurations you input into the entries sidebar panel are executed entirely within your own local browser instance. They are never sent to a backend server, never retained, and disappear completely if you close or refresh your tab.

3. Analytics & Cookies

To help keep our random generator application completely free, we may utilize minimal, privacy-compliant tracking or server monitoring tool sets to record generalized site views. This records aggregates like total visits or browser variants but never builds individualized behavior profiles or tracks you across external web entities.

4. Third-Party Web Hosting

Our web infrastructure is deployed via GitHub Pages platforms. While GitHub may track raw, technical networking logs necessary to securely host and distribute static HTML text structures, these systems do not map directly to marketing metrics controlled by us.

5. Updates and Contact

We reserve the right to refine or tweak this static operational manifest as we scale feature sets or security parameters. Any modifications will instantly update on this current canvas page layout.
