About Us

Simplifying decision making through clean, high-performance web engineering.

Our Mission

WheelPicker was built out of a simple realization: making everyday group decisions, classroom picks, or giveaway selections shouldn't require complex registration, heavy loading times, or intrusive tracking profiles. We set out to design a sleek, blazing-fast web application that handles randomized distribution perfectly while keeping everything executing securely right inside your web browser container.

Core Architecture

Unlike traditional online tools that cycle calculations through remote cloud servers, WheelPicker is engineered strictly client-side. This means every custom item label list, sound trigger, and canvas wheel slice calculation updates immediately, operating cleanly with zero server lag and absolute privacy footprint containment.
